01

Whipped cottage cheese toast with chili crisp

5 minutes 1 serving 28g protein
Ingredients
  • ·1 thick slice sourdough or seeded bread
  • ·3/4 cup (170g) cottage cheese
  • ·1 tsp chili crisp or hot honey
  • ·Flaky salt
  • ·Optional: a few cucumber ribbons
Method
  1. 01Toast the bread dark. Dark toast holds up under a wet topping.
  2. 02Blitz the cottage cheese for 20 seconds in a small blender, or mash it hard with a fork, until it turns glossy.
  3. 03Pile it on the toast, spoon the chili crisp over, finish with flaky salt.

Why it works. Cottage cheese whipped is a different food than cottage cheese scooped. Same tub, no shopping trip, 28 grams of protein.

02

Savory oats with a jammy egg

10 minutes 1 serving 24g protein
Ingredients
  • ·1/2 cup rolled oats
  • ·1 cup water or broth
  • ·1 egg
  • ·2 tbsp grated parmesan or cheddar
  • ·Scallion, soy sauce, black pepper
Method
  1. 01Boil water in a small pot. Lower one egg in for 7 minutes, then move it to cold water.
  2. 02Add the oats to the same water and cook 5 minutes, stirring, until thick.
  3. 03Stir in the cheese and a splash of soy. Top with the peeled egg, halved, plus scallion and a lot of pepper.

Why it works. Oats are only a sweet food by habit. Treated like risotto they carry cheese, egg and salt, and the pot count stays at one.

03

Freezer-berry yogurt bowl with toasted seeds

6 minutes 1 serving 26g protein
Ingredients
  • ·1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ·3/4 cup frozen berries
  • ·2 tbsp pumpkin or sunflower seeds
  • ·1 tsp honey or maple
  • ·Pinch of cinnamon
Method
  1. 01Toast the seeds dry in a small pan, 3 minutes, until they start popping.
  2. 02Microwave the frozen berries 45 seconds so they collapse into their own syrup.
  3. 03Spoon the berries over the yogurt, add the seeds and cinnamon, drizzle honey.

Why it works. Frozen berries make their own sauce, which is the part a fresh berry cannot do. This is also the cheapest 26 grams on the list.

Black bean and egg skillet, wrapped

9 minutes 2 servings 22g protein
Ingredients
  • ·1 can black beans, drained
  • ·2 eggs
  • ·2 flour tortillas
  • ·1/2 tsp cumin
  • ·Salsa, cheese, cilantro — whatever is open
Method
  1. 01Warm the beans in a dry skillet with the cumin, smashing about half of them.
  2. 02Push them aside, crack the eggs into the empty half, cover, 3 minutes.
  3. 03Warm the tortillas over the same burner, fill, add salsa, roll tight.

Why it works. One pan, one can, and the can was already in the cupboard. It doubles for two people without doubling the time.

05

Peanut banana shake, no protein powder

4 minutes 1 serving 21g protein
Ingredients
  • ·1 banana, ideally frozen
  • ·1 cup milk or kefir
  • ·2 tbsp peanut butter
  • ·1/2 cup cottage cheese or Greek yogurt
  • ·Pinch of salt, ice
Method
  1. 01Put everything in the blender, cottage cheese first so it sits against the blade.
  2. 02Blend 45 seconds. Longer than feels necessary — that is what makes it smooth instead of grainy.
  3. 03Salt it. A pinch of salt is the difference between this and a sad smoothie.

Why it works. Twenty-one grams of protein with nothing bought from a supplement aisle. Freeze bananas as they turn and this is a four-minute breakfast forever.

Questions

Asked and answered

What is the fastest high-protein breakfast?

The peanut banana shake, at about four minutes and 21 grams of protein. Everything goes in the blender at once and there is nothing to wash but the jar.

Can I make any of these the night before?

The savory oats and the yogurt bowl both hold. Cook the oats and refrigerate them, then loosen with a splash of water in the morning. For the yogurt bowl, keep the toasted seeds in a separate jar so they stay crisp.

How do I get to 30 grams of protein at breakfast?

Add one thing rather than making a bigger portion. An extra egg, two tablespoons of hemp seeds, or half a cup of cottage cheese each add roughly six to twelve grams without changing the recipe or the timing.
